# Delivery Methodologies

This document outlines **delivery methodologies** supported by the **Project Management (PM) Agent (Abbey)**. These methodologies provide frameworks for **planning, executing, and delivering** projects effectively.

---

## Agile
**Agile** is an iterative and incremental approach to project management, emphasizing **flexibility, collaboration, and customer feedback**.

### **Core Principles**
1. **Individuals and interactions** over processes and tools.
2. **Working software** over comprehensive documentation.
3. **Customer collaboration** over contract negotiation.
4. **Responding to change** over following a plan.

### **Frameworks**
#### **Scrum**
- **Roles**: Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team.
- **Artifacts**: Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment.
- **Events**: Sprint, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective.

#### **Kanban**
- **Visualize the workflow** (e.g., To Do, In Progress, Done).
- **Limit work in progress (WIP)** to improve flow.
- **Continuous improvement** through metrics (e.g., cycle time, lead time).

---

## Waterfall
**Waterfall** is a **linear, sequential approach** to project management, where each phase must be completed before the next begins.

### **Phases**
1. **Requirements**: Gather and document all requirements.
2. **Design**: Create a detailed design based on requirements.
3. **Implementation**: Develop the product or solution.
4. **Testing**: Verify the implementation against requirements.
5. **Deployment**: Release the product to users.
6. **Maintenance**: Address issues and updates post-deployment.

### **Pros and Cons**
| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Clear structure and milestones | Inflexible to changes |
| Easy to manage and understand | Late testing can lead to costly fixes |
| Well-documented | Not suitable for dynamic environments |

---

## Hybrid Approaches
Many projects use a **combination of methodologies** to balance structure and flexibility. For example:
- **Scrumban**: Combines Scrum and Kanban for flexibility in sprints.
- **Agile-Waterfall Hybrid**: Uses Agile for development and Waterfall for planning and milestones.

---

## Choosing a Methodology
| Factor | Agile | Waterfall | Hybrid |
|--------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Flexibility** | High | Low | Medium |
| **Customer Involvement** | High | Low | Medium |
| **Planning** | Iterative | Upfront | Balanced |
| **Risk Management** | Continuous | Late | Balanced |
| **Best For** | Dynamic environments, software development | Stable requirements, construction | Mixed environments |
